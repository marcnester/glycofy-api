# Beta legal readiness

The in-product Privacy Policy and Terms describe Glycofy's current technical behavior, including self-service data export and account deletion. They remain launch drafts until reviewed by qualified counsel.

Before inviting external beta testers:

- confirm the business/operator identity and contact information;
- review health, nutrition, allergy, and medical-advice disclaimers;
- confirm age eligibility and any parental-consent requirements;
- document retention periods for security and operational records;
- confirm subprocessors and cross-border data handling;
- review Google, Strava, OpenAI, hosting, analytics, and email-provider disclosures;
- confirm applicable privacy rights, consent, and response procedures by launch jurisdiction;
- record counsel approval and publish the approved effective date.

This checklist is operational guidance, not legal advice.
