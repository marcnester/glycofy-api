Glycofy Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026 · Launch draft pending legal review

Glycofy provides nutrition and training-planning tools. This policy explains the information Glycofy collects, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We collect account information such as your email address and display name; profile information you choose to provide, including dietary preferences, allergies, body measurements, goals, and unit preferences; generated meal plans and related activity; feedback you choose to send; privacy-safe product events; and technical security data such as request identifiers, timestamps, and privacy-preserving audit records.

Beta feedback automatically includes the Glycofy page path, coarse browser family, screen-size category, and request identifiers. It does not automatically include meal contents, macros, health profile fields, workouts, IP addresses, full browser identifiers, or URL query parameters. Product analytics uses a fixed set of journey events without free-form properties.

If you choose Google sign-in, we receive your Google account identifier, verified email address, name, and locale. Glycofy uses Google only to authenticate you and does not retain Google access or refresh tokens after sign-in. If you connect another service such as Strava, that service’s authorization screen describes the additional data requested.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, personalize nutrition and training plans, operate connected features you request, maintain security, diagnose failures, and improve reliability. Glycofy does not sell personal information or use health and dietary information for targeted advertising.

Service providers and AI

Information may be processed by vendors that provide hosting, database, authentication, connected-account, email, monitoring, and AI services. When you request AI-assisted planning, relevant preferences and planning context may be sent to the configured AI provider. Avoid entering information that is not needed for planning, and review AI output before use.

Retention and security

We retain account information while your account is active and retain limited security audit data according to our operational retention schedule. We use HTTPS, access controls, encrypted provider credentials, restricted production configuration, and security monitoring. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may decline Google sign-in and use another available sign-in method. From your Profile, you may disconnect connected services, download a copy of your Glycofy data, or permanently delete your account. Account deletion removes your profile, plans, training data, preferences, feedback, and locally stored connection credentials; Glycofy also attempts to revoke connected Strava access. You may additionally revoke access in the provider’s settings. Limited records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Health disclaimer and children

Glycofy is a planning tool, not a medical service, and is not intended to diagnose or treat any condition. Glycofy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service evolves and will revise the effective date when we do. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to marcnester@gmail.com.
